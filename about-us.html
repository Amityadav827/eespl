TREATMENT EXPERTISE

Engineering Capability Built Through Experience

Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP)

EESPL undertakes the comprehensive Design, Manufacture, Supply, Erection, and Commissioning of Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP) on a turnkey basis for diverse types and natures of wastewaters and industrial effluents. Our systems incorporate advanced primary, secondary, and tertiary polishing processes engineered specifically for the effective removal of organic, inorganic, oil and grease, heavy metals, and suspended solids across hotels, residential apartments, and demanding industrial facilities.

Every treatment scheme is engineered following thorough examination of effluent samples, extensive treatability studies, and evaluation of diverse treatment methods to guarantee operational practicability and cost-efficiency. We engineer compact, custom-made designs, and portable systems with small footprints and energy-saving operations. Additionally, we specialize in the up-gradation and modification of existing ETP systems to ensure full compliance with the statutory discharge standards set by the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB), New Delhi.

Sewage Treatment Plants (STP)

EESPL specializes in the turnkey design, fabrication, supply, erection, and commissioning of Sewage Treatment Plants (STP) engineered for the hotel industry, residential colonies, hospitals, IT parks, commercial buildings, and industrial units in metros and urban hubs. Each plant is meticulously engineered to comply strictly with environmental discharge standards mandated by State Pollution Control Boards (SPCBs) while ensuring reliable and economical day-to-day operation.

Addressing the critical challenges of high land costs and spatial constraints in metropolitan locations, our engineering team designs compact and modular STP installations built in accordance with consultant recommendations and site-specific needs. Utilizing the latest techno-commercially viable processes and systems (such as MBBR, SBR, MBR, and ASP), we deliver treated effluent of superior quality suitable for flushing, horticultural irrigation, cooling tower makeup, and industrial recycling.

CORPORATE SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY

People. Planet. Profit.

The phrase “people, planet, and profit” describing the triple bottom line and sustainability was coined by John Elkington in 1994. It highlights a responsible business philosophy where industrial advancement is kept in lasting equilibrium with social welfare and environmental preservation.

EESPL contributes its bit to environmental protection, helping improve the environment while encouraging sustainable development within the business. EESPL believes in the triple bottom line theory of People, Planet and Profit as a complete sustainable model, ensuring that every engineering solution contributes positively to our communities, our ecology, and long-term economic resilience.
